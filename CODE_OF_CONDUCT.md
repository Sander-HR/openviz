# Code of Conduct

OpenViz is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community.

Participants must:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact.
- Welcome different backgrounds and perspectives.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and unwanted contact.

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when conduct violates these standards. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers.
